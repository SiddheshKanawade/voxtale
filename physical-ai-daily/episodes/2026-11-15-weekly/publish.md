### supermimic-flips-imagination

**Title** (40 chars): `A robot that rehearses flips in its head`

**Description**
```
UC Berkeley's SuperMimic first learns how the robot's body moves, then practices inside that learned model, and the moves work on a real Unitree G1. These are the authors' own tests, on motions it had already practiced.
Sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28480 · https://supermimic.github.io
Video: Liao et al., https://supermimic.github.io
```

### supermimic-one-gpu

**Title** (35 chars): `Humanoid control trained on one GPU`

**Description**
```
After three hours on a single GPU, UC Berkeley's SuperMimic beat a leading controller that had trained for 7 days on 128 GPUs, and it ran on a real humanoid. This is the authors' own comparison, on motions it had already practiced.
Sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28480 · https://supermimic.github.io
Video: Liao et al., https://supermimic.github.io
```
