### 2. Apple: HALO humanoid trained in simulation

Status: **confirmed**. Primary: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06908

**Source quotes:**
- "Without ever seeing a real home during training, HALO transfers zero-shot to the physical world, achieving a 91% success rate across 360 trials in six unmapped real-world environments." (https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06908)
- "We present HALO, a humanoid autonomy system powered by a pipeline that synthesizes vision-language-motion data across a thousand procedurally generated homes." (https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06908)
- "Deployed on the physical robot, HALO achieves a 91% success rate over 360 trials across six real-world home environments that were neither seen during training nor scanned or mapped." (https://arxiv.org/html/2611.06908v1)
- "Our hardware platform is a Unitree G1 with two physical modifications: a custom head mount carrying an OAK-1W camera, and a rear enclosure housing a Mac mini M4 Pro (64 GB)." (https://arxiv.org/html/2611.06908v1)
- "The policy achieves high single-task transfer (91% sim, 91% real)." (https://arxiv.org/html/2611.06908v1)
- "An orchestrator (an off-the-shelf vision-language model invoked every two seconds) decomposes a free-form language request into a sequence of subtasks, each identifying one skill and the object it applies to." (https://arxiv.org/html/2611.06908v1)
- "with off-robot communication restricted to optional cloud VLM queries." (https://arxiv.org/html/2611.06908v1)
- "This repository contains the code for data generation, model training, and deployment from the HALO paper." (https://github.com/apple-aiml-research/ml-halo)
- "Affiliation: Apple" (https://arxiv.org/html/2611.06908v1)

**Not stated in the source:**
- The 91% figure is for single-task trials; the paper reports multi-task (long-horizon) sequences separately, with multi-task real-world evaluation in 1 real-world environment ("multi-task evaluation uses 50 simulated and 1 real-world environment").

- No independent replication is reported; all trials were run by the authors.
