### 3. Frontier AI models as robot policies: SenseNova test, LEXP, Luma ZAP

Status: **confirmed**. Primary: https://www.sensenova.ai/has-gpt-6-astra-achieved-embodied-intelligence

**Source quotes:**
- "We ask not only how capable GPT-6-Astra is, but what its capabilities imply for the field: we probe its limits on 12 benchmarks, dissect the harness behind it, validate it on eight real-robot platforms, and prototype what the next generation of embodied benchmarks should measure." (https://www.sensenova.ai/has-gpt-6-astra-achieved-embodied-intelligence)
- "GPT-6-Astra under a protocol that allows in-context learning on the training set and extends the execution budget up to 10×." (https://www.sensenova.ai/has-gpt-6-astra-achieved-embodied-intelligence)
- "It obtains 99–100% SR on six suites; the remaining errors concentrate in Long Task (97%) and Long Swap (90%)." (https://www.sensenova.ai/gpt-6/assets/docs/has_gpt-6_achieved_embodied_intelligence.pdf)
- "What limits success is the nature of the task: rigid pick-and-place is reliable (17/18 trials) and matches simulation, whereas deformable objects (e.g., cloth folding: 2/4 on the Franka 3), precise contact (e.g., plug insertion: 0/11 across three platforms) and long-horizon mobile tasks (e.g., navigation and search: 9/23 on the AgileX Tracer) remain markedly harder." (https://www.sensenova.ai/gpt-6/assets/docs/has_gpt-6_achieved_embodied_intelligence.pdf)
- "Taken together, our results suggest that GPT-6-Astra has largely crossed the threshold of embodied competence, but not yet that of practical embodied intelligence." (https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22471)
- "Across 12 real-world manipulation tasks, LEXP achieves 63.9% success, compared with 52.8% with memory alone and 5.6% for vanilla Astra." (https://arxiv.org/html/2610.41320v1)
- "We conduct experiments on a bimanual YAM robot equipped with two grippers and three view realsense cameras." (https://arxiv.org/html/2610.41320v1)
- "We introduce Zero-real Agent Policy (ZAP), a recipe that teaches this missing motion skill, entirely in simulation on simple primitive tasks and from the model's own successes, with no frontier teacher or distillation and no real-world robot data." (https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35817)
- "ZAP matches GPT-6 Astra (reasoning level max) in the same harness, each succeeding on 29 of 30 trials (Table 2), and both clearly outperform GPT-6 Astra in the Robocurve harness, MolmoAct2, and the base model." (https://arxiv.org/html/2610.35817v3)
- "We show that a small open VLM (Qwen 3.6 27B) already understands robot tasks and mainly lacks motion control, with only 36% of its motion calls correct in our harness (§3.2)." (https://arxiv.org/html/2610.35817v3)

**Not stated in the source:**
- SenseNova report primary date (arXiv 2610.22471 v1 submitted 15 Oct 2026, v2 19 Oct 2026) is outside the 2026-10-25..11-01 window; LEXP (submitted 30 Oct 2026) and ZAP (v1 26 Oct 2026) are inside it.
