# Channel list

Every channel from the 2026-11-15 competitor scan, with its link and numbers. Analysis is in `competitors.md`;
faceless channels in detail in `faceless-channels.md`.

- **Subs**: subscribers. **Median views / length**: last 30 long videos (Shorts excluded).
- **Uploads**: per week, from the dates of the latest and 10th-latest video.
- **Face**: whether the creator's face appears in the latest 4 thumbnails (checked by eye; a face can still appear
  inside videos).
